# Privacy policy

Effective date: 20 August 2026

CodeCanopy is a skills-only plugin containing text instructions. It does not include an MCP server, hosted service, analytics component, authentication flow, or independent data store, and it does not independently collect or transmit personal data.

When a user invokes CodeCanopy, the selected host, model, repository provider, and tools may process user prompts and authorized workspace content under their own terms and privacy policies. CodeCanopy instructs agents to minimize context, avoid secrets, and stay within the user's authorization.

Privacy questions may be opened through [GitHub issues](https://github.com/adhit-r/codecanopy/issues).
